## Product Management Intern — Summer 2027

**Meridian Software · San Francisco, CA (hybrid)**

### About the role

You'll join a product pod working on our analytics platform, partnering with
engineering, design, and data science to ship features used by thousands of daily
active users. This is a builder's internship — you'll own a feature area end to end,
from problem definition through launch and measurement.

### What you'll do

- Talk to customers and translate what you hear into problem statements the team
  can act on
- Define success metrics before you build, and be accountable to them after launch
- Write specs clear enough that engineers don't need to ask what you meant
- Prioritize ruthlessly against limited engineering capacity, and explain your
  reasoning to people who disagree
- Dig into product data yourself — SQL proficiency expected, not delegated
- Run launch readiness across support, marketing, and sales

### What we're looking for

- Currently pursuing a degree, graduating Dec 2027 – Jun 2029
- Evidence you've built or shipped something, in any context — coursework, a side
  project, a student org
- Comfort with data: you can pull your own numbers and know when a result is noise
- Clear written communication. You'll write more than you talk.
- Ability to hold a position under pushback, and to change it when the evidence
  changes

### Nice to have

- Prior internship in a product, analytics, or startup environment
- Familiarity with experimentation and A/B testing
- Experience working with engineers directly

### Details

12 weeks, starting June 2027. Applications reviewed on a rolling basis.
